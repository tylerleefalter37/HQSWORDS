📖 Kinetic Steel Combat Manual

1. The Three Measures (Mensur)

Zufechten (Out of Measure): You cannot strike without taking a passing or lunging step. Attacks from here telegraph heavily. Use this distance to void, recover stamina, or reset your guard.

Krieg (Striking Distance): Weapons can cross and strike with edge or point. Winding, master cuts, and active parries happen in this lethal window.

Handarbeit (Infighting): Distance collapses inside the blade's pivot. Crossguards, pommels, grappling, tripping, and dagger-thrusts dominate here.

2. Leverage & The Bind

The Strong (Forte): The half of the blade closest to the crossguard. Possesses maximum leverage.

The Weak (Foible): The half towards the point. Offers velocity and reach, but collapses against the forte.

Tactical Rule: If your Forte commands the opponent's Foible, your Master Cuts and thrusts gain devastating speed and cannot be brushed aside.

3. Anatomical Trauma Model

No generic hit point bars exist. Wounds are tracked specifically by tissue layer:

  • Throat & Carotid: Instant lethal exsanguination or asphyxiation.
  • Skull Trauma: Concussions over 80 Joules cause instant motor collapse or blackout.
  • Limb Tendons: Severed arm or leg tendons cripple weapon manipulation and topple balance to zero.
  • Blood Loss: Arterial wounds tick every turn; exceeding 60% induces circulatory shock.

4. Armor & Deflection Angles

Slashing heavy plate (Milanese, Gothic) with an edged sword merely chatters dull steel. Against rigid harness, fighters must deliver high-kinetic thrusts through openings (visor slits, maille voiders) or utilize blunt concussions (pommel strikes, war flails, poleaxes).
