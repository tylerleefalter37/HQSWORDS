📖 KINETIC STEEL CODEX (HISTORICAL HEMA MANUAL)
1. The Three Distances (Mensur)

Zufechten (Out of Measure): You cannot hit without stepping forward. Ideal for setting guards, baiting, or voiding back.
Krieg (Striking Measure): True fencing range. Edge, point, and binds happen here.
Handarbeit (Infighting): Clinch distance. Weapon pivots collapse. Pommel smashes, grappling, and dagger thrusts reign.

2. Leverage & The Bind

The Forte (Strong) near the hilt overpowers the Foible (Weak) near the tip. If you bind the enemy's weak blade with your strong, your Master Cuts and thrusts pierce through unhindered.

3. Secondary Off-Hand Weapons

Steel Buckler: High active deflection and brutal facial punch (Boselen).
Main Gauche: Traps enemy blades in quillons and counter-stabs.
Highland Targe: Spiked boss slam that shatters balance.
Fencing Cloak: Ensnare the enemy steel to disable their next attack.
Rondel Dagger: Slips into mail voiders and visor slits in close infighting.

4. Anatomical Trauma Model

Combatants have no abstract hit points. Carotid artery punctures trigger immediate arterial bleed. Skull concussions over 80 Joules cause immediate loss of consciousness. Severed leg tendons cause collapse to zero balance.
